### Level Two

1. Watch the next ten minutes of the video (up to 20:00).

2. Approximately how many rooms did Zork have on its map? <!-- 16:54 200-250 -->

3. What was the name of the company created to sell Zork? <!-- Infocom -->

3. Zork was made on a **mainframe** computer called a PDP-10.  Later it was made for **microcomputers** so people
could play it at home.  Why was it difficult to get it to work on microcomputers when it was already working
on the mainframe?  <!-- It was 1 megabyte program, too big for a 32 kilobyte microcomputer -->

### Level Three

1. Some of the slides were about part of the Zork program called the **parser**.
