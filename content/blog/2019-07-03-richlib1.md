Richlib is similar to Pygame Zero, but there are some differences.

You now have to put

    run()
    
at the end of your programs.

This calls a method in Richlib.  You can't see it, but what it does is something like this:

    def run():
        while True:
            update()
            draw()
            time.sleep(0.1)
