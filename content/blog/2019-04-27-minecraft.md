From our friends at Loughton Youth Project Coding Club:

Join us at The Space on the Friday of half term (May 31) for a Minecraft coding workshop.

Open to all aged 9-15, no experience of coding is required. we will start with simply building some things ourselves in creative mode and then move on to writing Python programs to build things automatically for us.

**Please bring your own Windows/Mac laptop with a copy of Minecraft Java Edition installed**, available [now at half-price](https://my.minecraft.net/store/minecraft/).  If you don’t have a laptop contact us before booking to arrange borrowing one of ours.

Please also bring a packed lunch, or lunch money and parental consent, if you plan to purchase lunch in Loughton during the lunch break (12-1pm).

Please book in advance as places are limited.

*Cost £10 on the day.*

Any enquiries email codingclub@loughton.me.uk
