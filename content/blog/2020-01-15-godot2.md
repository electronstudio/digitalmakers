## Scratch Beginners

If you have done little or no Scratch before, you will be doing these projects.

You don't have to print these but it might be easier if you do.  
Click on a project and then click the printer icon to get a version you can print-out.

1. [Deep sea sushi](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/projects/cd-beginner-scratch-sushi)
2. [Ghostbusters](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/projects/ghostbusters)
3. [Boat Race](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/projects/boat-race)
